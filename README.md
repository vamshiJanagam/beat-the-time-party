Beat the Time

**A fast-paced word party game for 2–8 players.** Create a room, invite your friends, and take turns naming words before the timer runs out. Avoid repeated words and forbidden starting letters to stay in the game.

## Play

[Play Beat the Time](https://beat-the-time-party.netlify.app)

Create a room and share its five-letter code, or join a friend’s room with their code. On your turn, enter a valid word that matches the category and avoids the forbidden letters.

## Features

- Real-time multiplayer for up to eight players
- 24 categories and 1,592 curated word entries
- Private rooms and open games
- Character selection and a 3D tabletop arena
- Host-controlled pause and resume
- Independent music and sound controls
- Server-validated turns, categories, and answers

## Technology

JavaScript · Three.js · Netlify Functions · Netlify Blobs
